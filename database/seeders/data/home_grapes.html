Answers to common questions.

Who is this CMS demo designed for?

Solo consultants, coaches, small teams, and agencies who want a clean site to showcase offers, results, and booking flows—editable without code.

Can I customize everything in the visual builder?

Yes. Sections, colors, copy, and images are fully editable in Admin → Pages. Drag blocks from Website Templates and Inazuma/Executra presets.

Does it include banners and news?

Yes. Main hero slider, sub-banners on About/News/Contact, and the news module are wired for live demos.

Can I integrate contact forms?

Contact pages support CMS lead forms with inquiry types and validation—route to your team from the backend.
